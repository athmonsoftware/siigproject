THE PROBLEM WE WORK ON

01 — 03

Paper compliance cannot respond to an emergency. People can.

SIIG was founded around a simple gap: organisations may meet requirements on paper while their teams remain unsure what to do in a real incident. Our work connects safety planning to practical action.
