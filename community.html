COMMUNITY MEDICAL SUPPORT INITIATIVE

THE INTENTION

Equip the first response.

Our community medical support initiative focuses on making essential emergency supplies and equipment more accessible to selected institutions and organisations.

Its goal is to improve immediate response capability in settings where assistance is important and resources may be limited.

01 / SUPPLIES

Medical essentials

Supplies considered include first aid kits, wound care materials, gloves, protective barriers, CPR face shields, and basic trauma supplies.

02 / EQUIPMENT

Emergency tools

Items considered include fire extinguishers, emergency signage, basic evacuation equipment, and safety instruction posters.

03 / PRIORITY

Where need is high

Focus settings include schools, community centres, non-profits, small workplaces, and public gathering facilities.

PARTNERSHIPS
