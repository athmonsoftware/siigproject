SIIG / OUR METHOD

Confidence comes from doing.

We believe safety training should change what people can do, not only what they can recall.

LEARN — PRACTISE — PERFORM

HOW WE TRAIN

From knowledge to instinct.

Our sessions use repeated practice and realistic scenarios. Each programme should reflect the environment where people will use the skills.

A THREE-PART APPROACH

01 / LEARN↗

Understand.

Recognise the situation, the risks, and the immediate priorities.

02 / PRACTISE✳

Repeat.

Work through the skill until the steps feel familiar under pressure.

03 / PERFORM→

Respond.

Apply learning in a realistic scenario and learn from the result.

“People remember what they do, not what they hear.”

SIIG / OUR TRAINING APPROACH

WHERE IT APPLIES

Preparedness changes with the setting.

Our training can serve corporate offices, schools, construction and industrial sites, warehouses, retail and hospitality, security companies, and community organisations. The method is adapted to each environment's real risks.
