THE OFFER

Prepare the people. Plan for the place.

Every setting has different risks. SIIG's work starts with what people need to be able to do, then connects learning and planning to practical response.

01 / TRAINING

First aid & fire safety.

Help your team practise the actions that matter in the first critical minutes.

Our hands-on, scenario-based sessions build confidence through repeated practice. Programmes can be shaped around a workplace, school, or group.

First aid topics

  • Basic first aid
  • CPR and AED use
  • Workplace and paediatric first aid
  • Emergency scene management
  • Refresher training

Fire safety topics

  • Fire awareness
  • Fire warden or marshal training
  • Extinguisher identification and use
  • Evacuation procedures
  • Practical drills

03 / EVENTS

Event safety & care.

Make a considered plan for the people gathering in your care.

Event support can include pre-event risk review, on-site first aid cover, communication coordination, incident documentation, and assistance for guests who need it. Staffing and service boundaries are agreed before an event.

Before the event

  • Event setting and audience review
  • Risk and response planning
  • Responder placement
  • Communication arrangements

On the day

  • First aid cover
  • Guest assistance
  • Mobility and non-clinical support for guests who need it
  • Emergency coordination
  • Incident documentation

We can discuss support for corporate events, school functions, community gatherings, sporting events, festivals, private celebrations, and religious events.

Support for people on site

Responders may assist with fainting and dehydration, cuts and minor injuries, allergic reactions, breathing difficulties, and other medical episodes while coordinating further help where needed. Depending on the agreed scope, guest care can also include non-clinical mobility assistance, medication reminders, heat and fatigue monitoring, and a calm place to rest.

Event fire and emergency support can include hazard checks, monitoring exits and crowd flow, and assisting with evacuation arrangements.
