SIIG / ABOUT

Safety is a culture of action.

SIIG exists to help people move from knowing the rules to being ready for the moment.

ESTABLISHED FEBRUARY 2025 — GHANA

WHY WE STARTED

From paper compliance to real preparedness.

Safety Innovations Impact Group was established in February 2025 in response to a gap its founders saw in workplaces: safety requirements can be met on paper while people remain unsure how to respond in an emergency.

Our work spans practical first aid and fire safety training, workplace support, and event safety services shaped around real environments and the people in them.

01 / VISION

Safer communities and workplaces.

A future where every individual is confident, capable, and prepared to respond effectively in an emergency.

02 / MISSION

Training that translates into action.

Deliver practical, engaging safety training that empowers people, reduces risk, helps prevent injuries, and can save lives.

WHAT GUIDES THE WORK

Five commitments, one standard.

The principles behind how we prepare people and places.

01

Preparedness

Training must translate into action.

02

Integrity

Compliance delivered honestly and correctly.

03

Impact

Every person trained can increase community safety.

04

05

Empowerment

Confidence matters alongside knowledge.

HOW SIIG WORKS

Practical by design.

We use realistic emergency scenarios, workplace-specific instruction, flexible on-site sessions, compliance-aligned programmes, and ongoing safety guidance.
